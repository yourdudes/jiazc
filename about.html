Home/About

The boring parts of importing? Those are the ones we take seriously.

JIAZC is a Hong Kong trading company helping importers and brands move apparel, toys and sporting goods out of southern China. We turn a tangle of factories, inspectors and forwarders into one relationship you can actually phone — and a real person who picks up.

Who we are

We sit between the factory floor and your shelves

We're based in Hong Kong, with our office in Fotan, Shatin and people on the ground across the cut-and-sew belt of the Pearl River Delta — close enough that we can be on a factory floor the same afternoon you flag a problem. We focus on three fast-moving categories: apparel, toys and sporting goods. For each of them, we're the one name you hold accountable instead of a dozen suppliers you've never met.

Placing your first trial order or running a recurring program, you get the same bilingual team handling sourcing, sampling, QC, compliance paperwork and logistics. We carry the operational load so you can stay focused on selling.

What drives us

Two things we'll put our name to

What we're here to do

Make sourcing from China simple, transparent and something you can rely on — whatever your order size. You get factory-direct apparel, toys and sporting goods, honest pricing, and a team that owns the outcome instead of passing the blame when something slips.

Where we're headed

We want to be the supplier you quietly recommend to another buyer over coffee — not because of a slogan, but because the goods keep landing correct, compliant and on time, order after order, year after year.

Our values

How we'll treat your business

What you can expect from us on every quote, every inspection, every delivery.

Straight with you

Honest pricing, realistic timelines, no costs buried in the fine print. If a sailing slips or a price moves, you hear it from us first — not from a tracking page or a surprise invoice.

We'd rather have the awkward call

We inspect independently at every stage. Given the choice, we'll hold a shipment and have one uncomfortable conversation with you now — rather than let a defect reach your shelves and cost you a customer later.

Dates you can build a season on

Clear updates and on-time delivery, so the promise date we give you is one you can safely repeat to your own customers — and plan a season around without crossing your fingers.

The third order beats the first

We're in it for the long haul. The more we learn your products and standards, the faster and smoother each order runs — so working with us gets easier, not harder, the longer you stay.

Why we started

A young company, built on three convictions

Hong Kong

Set up where trade gets done

We registered JIAZC in Hong Kong in 2025 for a simple reason: it sits right next to the Pearl River Delta factory belt yet runs on clear contracts, easy banking and currency international buyers already trust. You get mainland sourcing with a Hong Kong company behind it.

Focus

Three product lines, done properly

Rather than claim we can source anything, we picked three categories we genuinely know — apparel, toys and sporting goods — and learned the export safety paperwork that matters, like EN71 and ASTM F963, so goods clear customs instead of getting held at the border.

Proof

Inspection that answers to you, not the factory

From day one we built our QC around evidence: in-line and pre-shipment checks to an AQL you sign off, with photo reports in your inbox before you release the balance. The report you get isn't graded by the people being graded.

Today

Where we are now

We're a small, focused Hong Kong team sourcing across all three product lines — and we run the way we always meant to: you reach a real person, not a portal that emails you a ticket number.
