Who we are

Connecting factories and the world

JIAZC is a full-service import and export trading company headquartered near the manufacturing heartland of southern China. We specialize in three fast-moving consumer categories — apparel & garments, toys, and sporting goods — and act as the single point of accountability between our buyers and a vetted network of factories.

Whether you are a retailer placing a first order or an established brand managing recurring programs, our bilingual team manages sourcing, sampling, quality control, compliance documentation and logistics so you can focus on selling.

Our journey

Fifteen years of growing trade

2009

Founded in Shenzhen

JIAZC began as a small apparel export desk, helping overseas buyers source garments directly from Guangdong factories.

2013

Added toys division

Responding to buyer demand, we launched a dedicated toys division with a focus on EN71 / ASTM safety compliance.

2017

Sporting goods & QC team

We expanded into fitness, balls and outdoor gear and built an in-house quality-control team for independent inspections.

2021

Logistics & DDP service

Door-to-door delivery and DDP options were introduced, giving buyers a fully managed import experience.

2026

40+ countries and growing

Today JIAZC serves buyers across five continents, shipping over 1,200 orders a year across three product divisions.
