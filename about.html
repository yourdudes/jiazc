A small trading house that takes the boring parts seriously

Since 2009 we have helped importers and brands move apparel, toys and sporting goods out of southern China — turning a tangle of factories, inspectors and forwarders into one relationship you can phone.

Who we are

Between the factory and the world

JIAZC is a full-service import and export trading company in Guangzhou's Tianhe district, a short drive from the cut-and-sew belt of the Pearl River Delta. We specialize in three fast-moving consumer categories — apparel & garments, toys, and sporting goods — and act as the single point of accountability between buyers and a vetted network of factories.

Whether you are a retailer placing a first order or an established brand managing recurring programs, our bilingual team handles sourcing, sampling, quality control, compliance documentation and logistics — so you can stay focused on selling.

Our journey

How we got here

2009

Founded in Guangzhou

JIAZC started as a two-person apparel export desk, helping overseas buyers source garments directly from Guangdong factories instead of through layers of agents.

2013

Added the toys desk

Buyers kept asking, so we built a dedicated toys desk and learned export safety paperwork properly — EN71 and ASTM F963 — rather than improvising it.

2017

Sporting goods & an in-house QC team

We expanded into fitness, balls and outdoor gear, and stopped outsourcing inspection — building our own QC staff so the people checking the goods answer to us, not the factory.

2021

Door-to-door & DDP

We added managed DDP delivery for buyers who wanted nothing to do with freight and customs — receive the goods, nothing else to arrange.

2026

Where we are now

Today JIAZC ships to buyers on five continents across three product divisions, still run as a desk you can reach a person at — not a portal.
