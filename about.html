The boring parts of importing? Those are the ones we take seriously.

Since 2009 we've helped importers and brands move apparel, toys and sporting goods out of southern China. We turn a tangle of factories, inspectors and forwarders into one relationship you can actually phone — and a real person who picks up.

Who we are

We sit between the factory floor and your shelves

Our desk is in Guangzhou's Tianhe district, a short drive from the cut-and-sew belt of the Pearl River Delta — close enough that we can be on a factory floor the same afternoon you flag a problem. We focus on three fast-moving categories: apparel, toys and sporting goods. For each of them, we're the one name you hold accountable instead of a dozen suppliers you've never met.

Placing your first trial order or running a recurring program, you get the same bilingual team handling sourcing, sampling, QC, compliance paperwork and logistics. We carry the operational load so you can stay focused on selling.

Our journey

How we got here

2009

We started with two people and one idea

We opened as a two-person apparel export desk with a simple pitch: let overseas buyers source garments straight from Guangdong factories, instead of paying through layers of agents who'd never seen the line.

2013

Buyers kept asking for toys, so we did it right

Our garment buyers kept asking us to source toys too. So we built a dedicated toys desk and learned the export safety paperwork properly — EN71, ASTM F963 — rather than winging it and hoping the goods cleared.

2017

Sporting goods — and we brought QC in-house

We added fitness gear, balls and outdoor equipment — and made a call that still defines us: we stopped outsourcing inspection. Our own QC staff answer to us, not to the factory, so the report you get isn't graded by the people being graded.

2021

Door-to-door, fully managed (DDP)

For buyers who wanted nothing to do with freight, duties or customs, we added fully managed DDP: we handle everything to your door, you just receive the goods. It's now how most first-time buyers start with us.

2026

Where we are now

Today we ship to buyers on five continents across all three product lines — and we still run the way we started: a team you reach a real person at, not a portal that emails you a ticket number.
