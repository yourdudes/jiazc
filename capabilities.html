Understanding Incoterms

Choose the terms that fit your operation

FOB — Free On Board

We deliver goods cleared for export and loaded onto your nominated vessel. You manage main freight and insurance — ideal if you have your own forwarder.

CIF — Cost, Insurance & Freight

We arrange and pay for ocean freight and insurance to your destination port. A balanced option that simplifies shipping while you handle import clearance.

DDP — Delivered Duty Paid

The fully managed option: we handle freight, duties and delivery to your door. You receive the goods with nothing more to arrange — the simplest way to import.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

What is your typical minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs vary by product and division — generally 300–500 pieces for apparel, 500–1,000 for toys, and 100–500 for sporting goods. For new buyers we can often arrange trial quantities; tell us your target and we'll advise what's workable.

Which Incoterms and payment methods do you support?

We quote on FOB, CIF or DDP terms. Common payment arrangements include T/T (telegraphic transfer) with a deposit and balance, and letter of credit (L/C) for established programs. Exact terms are agreed in writing before any order is placed.

Can you help with toy safety and textile compliance?

Yes. We coordinate testing through accredited third-party laboratories to support standards such as EN71 and ASTM F963 for toys, plus textile labelling and material declarations. We provide compliance as a capability and align documentation to your destination market's requirements.

How long does production usually take?

After sample approval, typical lead times are 30–45 days for apparel, 35–60 days for toys (including any tooling), and 30–50 days for sporting goods. Peak seasons and complex customization can extend these; we always confirm a realistic schedule in your quotation.

Do you offer samples before bulk production?

Always. We arrange pre-production samples so you can confirm materials, sizing, colours and packaging before mass production. Sample fees are typically charged and, for most apparel and sporting goods, can be credited against a confirmed bulk order.

Which markets do you ship to?

We export to over 40 countries across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Latin America and Oceania, shipping primarily from Shenzhen, Ningbo and Shanghai. If your destination isn't listed, just ask — we can almost always arrange it.
