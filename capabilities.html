The parts of an import that go wrong — and how we hold them

A border crossing has dozens of moving parts. We bring four of them together — sourcing, quality, compliance and logistics — under one accountable team, with terms you can plan a season around.

Core capabilities

What you actually get from a trade partner

01

Sourcing & supplier audits

We work from a vetted base of factories across the Pearl River Delta and beyond. Before recommending anyone, we check capability, capacity and conduct in person, then negotiate pricing on your behalf in Chinese.

02

Quality control

Our in-house QC staff run in-line and pre-shipment inspections against an AQL agreed with you before production, and send photographic reports before you authorize shipment — so you decide with evidence, not assurances.

03

Compliance support

We help you meet destination requirements — coordinating toy safety testing (EN71 / ASTM F963), textile labelling and material declarations through accredited third-party laboratories. We offer this as a capability and align documents to your market; we don't sell certificates.

04

Logistics & freight

Sea, air and express options with consolidation, container loading supervision and customs documentation, shipped under your chosen Incoterms — mainly from Nansha, Shekou and Yantian.

05

A real global footprint

We have shipped to buyers across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Latin America and Oceania in the last two years. If your destination isn't one we've served, we'll tell you plainly and find a route.

06

Trade terms in writing

FOB, CIF and DDP with transparent, itemized quotations. Payment is typically T/T with a deposit and balance, or L/C for established programs — agreed up front, never improvised mid-order.

Quality assurance

Inspected before it ships — every time

Quality is not a final-step formality here. Our inspectors check incoming materials, watch production in-line, and run a final pre-shipment inspection against the sampling plan we agreed with you — the same plan, every order.

  • Defined AQL levels — agreed with you before production starts, not after a problem.
  • Photo & video reports — documented evidence in your hands before you release the balance.
  • Lab testing coordination — EN71, ASTM and textile tests via accredited partners.

Understanding Incoterms

Choose the terms that fit your operation

FOBFree On Board

We deliver goods cleared for export and loaded onto your nominated vessel at Nansha or Shekou. You manage main freight and insurance — ideal if you already have a forwarder you trust.

CIFCost, Insurance & Freight

We arrange and pay ocean freight and insurance to your destination port. A balanced middle ground that simplifies shipping while you handle import clearance on your side.

DDPDelivered Duty Paid

The fully managed option: we handle freight, duties and delivery to your door. You receive the goods with nothing left to arrange — the simplest way to import, and the one most first-time buyers choose.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

What is your typical minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

It depends on the product and division — generally 300–500 pieces for apparel, 500–1,000 for toys, and 100–500 for sporting goods. For new buyers we can often arrange trial quantities; tell us your target and we'll advise honestly what's workable with the factory.

Which Incoterms and payment methods do you support?

We quote on FOB, CIF or DDP. Payment is usually T/T (telegraphic transfer) with a deposit and balance against inspection, and letter of credit (L/C) for established programs. Exact terms are agreed in writing before any order is placed.

Can you help with toy safety and textile compliance?

Yes — as a coordination capability. We arrange testing through accredited third-party laboratories to support standards such as EN71 and ASTM F963 for toys, plus textile labelling and material declarations, and align documentation to your destination market. We facilitate testing rather than issue certificates ourselves.

How long does production usually take?

After sample approval, typical lead times are 30–45 days for apparel, 35–60 days for toys (including any tooling), and 30–50 days for sporting goods. Peak season and complex customization extend these — we'll confirm a realistic schedule in your quotation rather than an optimistic one.

Do you offer samples before bulk production?

Always. We arrange pre-production samples so you can confirm materials, sizing, colours and packaging before mass production. Sample fees are typically charged and, for most apparel and sporting goods, credited against a confirmed bulk order.

Which markets do you ship to?

In the last two years we've shipped to over 38 countries across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Latin America and Oceania, loading mainly from Nansha, Shekou and Yantian. If your destination isn't one we've served, just ask — we can almost always arrange it.
