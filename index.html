How we work

From inquiry to delivery in four clear steps

A structured, transparent process that keeps you informed at every milestone.

1

Inquiry & quote

Share your product, target specs and volumes. We respond with sourcing options and a clear FOB/CIF quotation.

2

Sampling & approval

We arrange samples, confirm materials, sizing and packaging, and lock specifications before production begins.

3

Production & QC

Manufacturing is monitored with in-line checks and a 100% pre-shipment inspection, documented with photo reports.

4

Logistics & delivery

We handle booking, customs documentation and shipping under your chosen Incoterms — to port, warehouse or door.
