What we do

One desk between your order and the factory floor

Most importers end up coordinating a factory, a forwarder, an inspector and a customs broker themselves. We do that work, in Chinese, on the ground in Guangzhou — so you place one order and receive one accountable answer.

We are not a marketplace and we are not a factory. We are the people who walk the production line, hold a shipment when it isn't right, and keep your costs itemized and honest.

How an order runs

From your first email to goods on the water

01

Inquiry & quote

Send specs, target price and quantity. We reply with sourcing options and an itemized FOB or CIF quote, usually within two business days.

02

Sampling & sign-off

We arrange pre-production samples, lock materials, sizing and packaging, and agree the AQL before anyone cuts bulk.

03

Production & QC

In-line checks during the run and a pre-shipment inspection at the end, both documented with photos before you release the balance.

04

Booking & export

We book space, supervise loading, prepare customs documents and ship under FOB, CIF or DDP — from Nansha, Shekou or Yantian.
