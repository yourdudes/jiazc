How an order runs

From your first email to goods on the water

01

Inquiry & quote

Send your specs, target price and quantity. You get sourcing options and an itemized FOB or CIF quote back — usually within two business days.

02

Sampling & sign-off

You approve a pre-production sample. We lock materials, sizing and packaging and agree the AQL — before anyone cuts bulk.

03

Production & QC

We check the line during the run and inspect again before shipment — both backed by photos you see before you release the balance.

04

Booking & export

We book space, supervise loading, prepare the customs documents and ship under FOB, CIF or DDP from Nansha, Shekou or Yantian. You just receive the goods.
