Apparel & Garments

Seasonal fashion, knitwear, uniforms, workwear — we source it from knitting mills, cut-and-sew factories and fabric suppliers across Guangdong and Zhejiang. The hard part is keeping sizing and shade consistent across colours, sizes and reorders, and that's exactly where we hold the line. Your second run won't surprise you.

Bring your own tech pack and labels for OEM, or hand us a reference and a target price and let our development team build a private-label range from scratch. Either way, you sign off on a sample before bulk.

  • CategoriesFashion apparel, knitwear, uniforms, workwear, fabrics, accessories
  • ServicesOEM / ODM, private label, sampling, size grading
  • Typical MOQ300–500 pcs per style / colour
  • Lead time30–45 days after sample approval
  • Trade termsFOB · CIF · DDP

02 · Toys
Division 02

Toys

Fun product, serious paperwork — toys only ship if both are right. We source plush, wooden educational, plastic and simple electronic toys from Chenghai factories that already export, and line up EN71 (EU) and ASTM F963 (US) testing through accredited third-party labs when you need it. The goal is simple: your toys clear customs, not sit in a bonded warehouse.

Retail plush or STEM kits for education buyers — we sort tooling, packaging artwork and age-grading documentation while production runs, not as a panic at the dock. So nothing holds up your shipment at the last minute.

  • CategoriesPlush, wooden educational, plastic, electronic toys
  • ComplianceEN71 / ASTM F963 testing coordination via accredited labs
  • Typical MOQ500–1,000 pcs per item
  • Lead time35–60 days including tooling
  • Trade termsFOB · CIF · DDP

Sporting Goods

This gear gets used hard, so we source it to survive use — not just the catalogue photo. Fitness equipment, stitched and inflatable balls, outdoor gear and team kit, for retailers, clubs and institutional buyers who can't have a ball burst or a seam blow mid-season.

Sublimated kit or home-gym equipment, we handle materials, custom logos and packaging — and we point QC at the things that actually fail: stitch strength, finishing and safe materials.

  • CategoriesFitness equipment, balls, outdoor gear, team sportswear
  • CustomizationLogos, sublimation, custom colours & packaging
  • Typical MOQ100–500 pcs depending on item
  • Lead time30–50 days after approval

What you get with every order

We're not just passing your email to a factory — this is the work that turns a quote into goods you can actually put on a shelf.

01

We find the right factory

We track down and vet factories that fit your spec, target price, MOQ and timeline, then negotiate for you in Chinese — face to face, not by forwarding a translated email and hoping for the best.

02

We check it before you pay for it

We check the line during production and inspect again before shipment, against an AQL we agreed with you, all backed by photos. Nothing leaves the factory unverified, and you release the balance only once you've seen the proof.

03

We get it to your door

Consolidation, booking, loading supervision and customs paperwork under FOB, CIF or DDP — to your port, warehouse or front door, loading from Nansha, Shekou or Yantian. Pick your Incoterm and we handle the rest.
