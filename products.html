Division 01

Apparel & Garments

From seasonal fashion to corporate workwear, our apparel division sources garments that fit, last and arrive on trend. We work with knitting mills, cut-and-sew factories and fabric suppliers to deliver consistent quality across colours, sizes and production runs.

OEM and ODM are both available — bring your tech pack and labels, or let our development team build a private-label range from your concept.

Categories
Fashion apparel, knitwear, uniforms, fabrics, accessories
Services
OEM / ODM, private label, sampling, grading
Typical MOQ
300–500 pcs per style / colour
Lead time
30–45 days after sample approval

Division 02

Toys

Our toys division pairs playful design with serious safety. We source plush, educational, plastic and electronic toys from factories experienced in international compliance, and we can coordinate testing to standards such as EN71 and ASTM F963 on request.

Whether you need licensed-style plush for retail or STEM kits for education buyers, we manage tooling, packaging artwork and age-grading documentation alongside production.

Categories
Plush, educational, plastic, electronic toys
Compliance
EN71 / ASTM F963 testing coordination
Typical MOQ
500–1,000 pcs per item
Lead time
35–60 days incl. tooling

Division 03

Sporting Goods

Built for performance and built to last — our sporting goods division covers fitness equipment, inflatable and stitched balls, outdoor gear and team sportswear. We help retailers, clubs and institutional buyers source durable products that stand up to real use.

From sublimated team kits to home-gym equipment, we coordinate materials, custom logos and packaging, with QC focused on stitching strength, finishing and safe materials.

Categories
Fitness equipment, balls, outdoor gear, team sportswear
Customization
Logos, sublimation, custom colours & packaging
Typical MOQ
100–500 pcs depending on item
Lead time
30–50 days after approval
